Ageing Navigator
Free Family Ageing Questionnaire
Tell us what's happening, and we'll help you work out what to do next.
Ageing can become complicated quickly.
You may be dealing with a fall, hospital stay, changes in memory or mobility, increasing care needs, questions about funding, or uncertainty about whether staying at home is still the right option.
Or things may be going well and you simply want to make sure the right plans are in place before something changes.
This questionnaire helps us prepare your free personalised Family Ageing Action Plan.
Depending on your situation, it should take around 10–15 minutes. You will only be shown questions that are relevant to you.
You don't need to know every answer. “Not sure” is useful information too.
Completing this for someone else?
That's completely fine.
The questionnaire can be completed:
By the older person themselves
By a family member or friend
Together with the older person
By someone helping them because they do not use, or cannot easily access, a computer
Answer based on what you know. You do not need to have every detail.
Please don't include account numbers, passwords, detailed medical records or other highly confidential information.
Important: Ageing Navigator provides independent ageing-related information and navigation. It does not provide medical diagnoses, legal or financial advice, or formal government needs assessments. Where specialist assessment or advice may be required, your Action Plan will explain who you may need to speak with.
If someone is in immediate danger or requires urgent medical attention, please contact the appropriate emergency service.

FINISH
Thank you — that's everything we need.
Your answers will be used to prepare your personalised Family Ageing Action Plan.
Rather than giving you a long list of generic information, the plan should focus on the areas that actually relate to your situation.
DO NOW
Anything requiring attention first, including urgent safety issues, important assessments or time-sensitive decisions.
DO NEXT
The most useful practical actions to take after the immediate priorities.
PLAN AHEAD
Things that may not require action today but are worth putting in place before circumstances change.
WHO TO CONTACT
Relevant New Zealand organisations, professionals, services and official information.
QUESTIONS TO ASK
Useful questions to take to your GP, hospital team, needs assessor, lawyer, village, residential-care provider or other professional.
OPTIONS THAT MAY FIT
Where appropriate, Ageing Navigator may identify providers, services, retirement villages or other options whose available information appears to match the needs and preferences you've told us about.
Matches should explain why they were selected and, where possible, when the provider information was last verified.
Providers cannot pay to be ranked higher or to be presented as the preferred option.
YOUR FAMILY SITUATION SUMMARY
The Action Plan should also include a concise one-page summary of the important information you've provided, so you do not have to explain the entire situation from the beginning every time.
You may choose to share this with people such as:
Family members
Your GP
Hospital or discharge teams
Needs assessors
Social workers
Home-care services
Retirement villages
Residential-care providers
The family remains in control of who receives it.

Pilot Feedback
Ageing Navigator is currently being tested with a limited number of families.
After you receive your Action Plan, we may ask you to complete a short feedback survey so we can improve the questionnaire and make the plans more useful for other New Zealand families.
